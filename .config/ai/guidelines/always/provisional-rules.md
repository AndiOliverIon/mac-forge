# Provisional Rule Lifecycle

- Human review feedback is evidence, not automatically a rule. Artanis, Argus, and Aegis may
  recommend candidates, but none may admit, remove, or promote one without Oliver's explicit
  decision.
- Before writing a provisional rule, recommend yes or no with the exact rule and chapter. Do not
  write a rule until Oliver explicitly confirms it after that recommendation, even when he initially
  asked to add it. If rejected, write nothing. If the proposal conflicts with existing guidance,
  debate and resolve the conflict before admission.
- An admitted provisional rule is immediately mandatory within its chapter. "Provisional" describes
  its maturity, not its enforcement strength.
- For any proposal, evaluation, addition, edit, removal, or promotion of provisional guidance, first
  read `~/.config/ai/guidelines/rule-lifecycle.md` and follow it.
